# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in TemporalThread a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We will act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, constructive feedback, respect for differing viewpoints, accountability, and attention to the community’s best interests. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, sexualized language or attention, personal or political attacks, trolling, insults, doxxing, and other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner’s GitHub profile. Maintainers will respect the reporter’s privacy, investigate in good faith, and take action proportionate to the circumstances. Enforcement may range from a warning to temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces.
